How to play

Trace streets from START to CALL. Release to pause; drag elsewhere to pan the map. Backtrack along your blue route to erase, or use Undo.

For tap or keyboard input, choose streets and select a direction at each connected junction. Each choice follows that street to its next decision. Undo removes the last choice. Selecting a return direction adds travel.

CALL marks public-road access to the address. Routes must obey mapped one-way streets. Review compares distance with a shortest legal route.
