Training, Privacy & Legal Notice

Emergency Games is an independent education, familiarization, practice and simulation product. It is not an operational emergency-response system.

Do not use this software for live emergency response.It is not for emergency navigation, dispatch, CAD replacement, route guidance, tactical decisions, operational decision-making or real-time incident management.

Use department-approved information for real incidents

For any real-world response, rely on your department's approved dispatch/CAD systems, maps, policies, procedures, communications, officer direction and other authorized operational resources.

Simulation data can be incomplete or outdated

  • Roads, addresses, stations, hospitals, map imagery, routing, travel times and other geographic information may be inaccurate, incomplete, delayed or outdated.
  • Displayed routes or response times are training aids only and are not representations of the safest, fastest, permitted or department-approved real-world response.
  • Game behaviour and scoring are designed for training and should not be interpreted as operational recommendations.

All incidents are simulated

Dispatch calls and incident descriptions in the public training database are fictional training scenarios and are not statements that an actual incident, patient, occupant or emergency occurred at a displayed location.

Business and organization names are used as geographic landmarks. Their appearance does not imply affiliation, endorsement or that the fictional incident actually occurred at that business or organization.

Sensitive fictional incidents may be displayed as occurring “outside / near” an identifiable landmark while preserving the real street/address context needed for geographic training.

Privacy and analytics

Product analytics are designed to measure how the simulator is used rather than create individual firefighter profiles. Depending on deployment settings, aggregate/session information can include department/deployment ID, play time, device/browser category, city and Fire/EMS use, calls, aggregate response timing, driving totals, controls/features used and reliability events.

The v1.6.37 privacy policy removes legacy persistent cross-visit player/browser analytics identifiers. Normal product analytics are not intended to store names, emails, exact driven routes/coordinate histories, prompts, room codes or real CAD/patient/incident data.

Department/private deployments default analytics off unless explicitly enabled for that deployment. Administrative analytics require an authenticated secure integration; the public website no longer treats a hidden URL or localStorage flag as administrator authentication.

The public Geo Guesser leaderboard is separate from product analytics. Players should use a nickname/alias rather than a real full name, and department/private deployments disable the public leaderboard by default.

Local policy takes priority

Departmental SOPs/SOGs, applicable law, road conditions, closures, emergency-scene conditions and instructions from authorized personnel always take priority over anything shown in this software.

Independent project and branding

Unless expressly stated otherwise for a specific deployment, this project is not an official dispatch, navigation or operational product of a named municipality, fire service, paramedic service, emergency communications centre or other agency, and no endorsement should be inferred.

Maps, data and third-party services

Maps, imagery, open data and software components can be supplied by third parties and remain subject to their applicable licences, service terms and attribution requirements.
