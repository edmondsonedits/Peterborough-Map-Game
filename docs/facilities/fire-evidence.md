## Station 2 — 100 Marina Boulevard

The City identifies the current station at 100 Marina Boulevard; it opened in October 2024. The inspected JCB project photos show three red, glazed four-fold apparatus openings across dark gray masonry, a broad dark fascia/clerestory band, a tall tower at the front-left, and a lower side wing. Timber accents appear in the project materials. These views support the front, but do not show a complete rear or side elevation. Reference set: `CoP_FireStationNo2_008.webp`, `009`, `013`, `028`, and `029` in the external research image folder. [JCB project photos](https://www.jcb.ca/en/project/fire-station-no2-at-peterborough); [City Fire Services](https://www.peterborough.ca/living-in-peterborough/fire-services/); [opening report](https://kawarthanow.com/2024/10/31/peterboroughs-new-11-million-fire-station-no-2-officially-opens-on-marina-boulevard/).

Northern Dock Systems specifies three 14×14 ft glazed four-fold doors and two 14×14 ft sectional doors with full-view polycarbonate sections. The three front openings are visible; the additional two doors’ location is not established. Their rear placement in the asset is inferred, not photo-confirmed. The model’s masonry/timber textures and small generic shield mark are original; the badge is inspired by the photographed crest silhouette and is not a verified reproduction of official arms. [Installer project note](https://www.northerndocksystems.com/peterboroughs-first-zero-carbon-building/). The 2022 City design package is design context, not as-built proof: [City project page](https://www.connectptbo.ca/fire).

## Station 3 — 839 Clonsilla Avenue

The City directory identifies 839 Clonsilla Avenue as the current Station 3; it is distinct from the former Monaghan Road station. Two official City photos dated 2018-07-31 were inspected:

- Photo 2, [official image](https://www.peterborough.ca/media/55fdhfjh/peterborough-fire-services_2018_07_31_city_peterborough_2018_2.jpg): angled front view with three nearly fully glazed sectional doors, silver frames, dark gray masonry, opaque light-gray vertical metal siding above the bay block, projecting fascia, and a red-clad entrance block with station/address lettering. A short side return is visible.
- Photo 44, [official image](https://www.peterborough.ca/media/e1bkuuz0/peterborough-fire-services_2018_07_31_city_peterborough_2018_44.jpg): closer front/corner view with the same door and masonry treatment and a large upper louver on the visible bay endwall.

The photos do not show the complete sides or rear. Rear wall details and door count remain unknown; the model uses a conservative generic perimeter there. GIS outline and anchor are retained from `contracts/station3-gis-contract.json`. [City Fire Services](https://www.peterborough.ca/living-in-peterborough/fire-services/).
