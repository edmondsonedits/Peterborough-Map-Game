# EMS base exterior evidence

Research date: 2026-10-27. The two bases are in Peterborough, Ontario. The local photos inspected for these notes remain in `H:\ChatGPT\Peterborough-Facilities-20261027\research\ems\images\`; they are reference material only and are not embedded as textures.

## 310 Armour Road — Headquarters

The County lists Peterborough Headquarters (Armour Road) with two 24-hour and three 12-hour ambulances and identifies the site at 310 Armour Road. [County service commitment](https://www.ptbocounty.ca/county-government/departments/paramedics/service-commitment/); [County 2023 service update](https://pub-douro-dummer.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=16535).

An inspected Global News photo published 2023-07-27 shows the headquarters sign and only a small portion of the entrance: red brick, glass entry, and a pale gray-blue canopy/roof edge. It does not establish the whole building, roof surface, any apparatus doors, or side/rear elevations. Local reference: `armour-global-sign-2023.jpg`. [Global News report](https://globalnews.ca/news/9860539/peterborough-paramedics-union-staff-shortage-risks/). Do not turn the ambulance staffing count into an inferred door count.

## 1003 Clonsilla Avenue — Station 7

The County’s Community Paramedicine page photo visibly identifies “Peterborough Paramedics — Station 7 — 1003 Clonsilla.” It supports a broad glazed storefront with silver mullions, light fascia/parapet, blue/orange lower bands, and a pale low roof edge. The lower facade is partly obscured; no side/rear wall or overhead-door location is shown. Local reference: `clonsilla-cp-program-vehicles.webp`. [County photo/page](https://www.ptbocounty.ca/living-in-our-community/emergency-services/community-paramedicine-and-health-monitoring/).

A 2017 opening story says the base was converted from an auto-sales building to include two vehicle bays, office and training space. This verifies the two-bay program, not where operational doors appear on the exterior. [The Millbrook Times, 2017-05-05](https://themillbrooktimes.ca/new-paramedic-station-expected-improve-response-times/).
