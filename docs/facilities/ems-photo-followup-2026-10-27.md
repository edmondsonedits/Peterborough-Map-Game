## Outcome and scope

This pass covered both city EMS bases using 95 requested Exa search-result slots across two address workstreams, supplementary exact-term/image searches, original County images, and manual inspection of dated Google Street View panoramas. Result slots include duplicates and irrelevant matches; they are not 95 independently verified sources. ## Clonsilla — 1003 Clonsilla Avenue / Station 7

### Strong photographic evidence

[September 2021 Google Street View](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=YhKp1v44kxUww_U4FWJ3fw&heading=279.63&pitch=0&fov=15), panorama `YhKp1v44kxUww_U4FWJ3fw`, camera 44.2894929,-78.3450205, was opened and visually inspected. The camera-location label is 1034 Clonsilla, across the road; the building being inspected is identified by the Station 7 / 1003 Clonsilla fascia and its match to the County photo. Do not mistake the camera label for the subject address.

Visible facts:
- Two separate white sectional garage doors are side by side in the recessed wing to the **viewer-left when facing the signed storefront from Clonsilla**.
- Both leaves have a narrow horizontal dark glazed band around mid-height. White opaque panel fields dominate the doors, with pale framing between the openings.
- The garage block has a broad light fascia. The nearer glazed storefront projects ahead of it, partly occluding the right-hand garage door from this camera position.
- The storefront uses silver frames, a pale fascia, and blue/orange bands. Door dimensions, exact panel count, hidden jambs and rear/internal circulation are not measured by this image.

[County Community Paramedicine photo/page](https://www.ptbocounty.ca/living-in-our-community/emergency-services/community-paramedicine-and-health-monitoring/) directly identifies the same frontage. [Original County image](https://www.ptbocounty.ca/media/0ntdpljy/cp-program-vehicles.png?format=webp&height=674&width=899) was inspected by the research agent. [The Millbrook Times opening report, 5 May 2017](https://themillbrooktimes.ca/new-paramedic-station-expected-improve-response-times/) was fetched in full and documents conversion of the former Paul Tinney Auto Sales premises to two vehicle bays, office and training areas. It does not give room dimensions or rear-door placement.

### Date/current-state limitation

July 2024 panoramas in the same parking/access area show a dark-clad building and construction. The initial search/address result by itself was not sufficient to identify a finished current EMS elevation. The parent followed the linked panoramas to the street and compared 2021 imagery; the dated 2021 Station 7 signage is the strong identity anchor for the door correction. Do not assert that the 2021 finish is a verified 2026 exterior, that EMS relocated, or that the black cladding belongs to a verified final EMS refurbishment without further address- and date-specific evidence. The 2025 County tender still lists a Clonsilla base but gives no door layout on its public summary.

**Model correction:** preserve the existing two closed doors on the recessed viewer-left wing, GIS placement and dimensions; replace the dark leaves with original white sectional geometry and a single narrow glazing band. Count/side/appearance are photo-backed for September 2021; dimensions and other elevations remain estimated. No Google or County image pixels are embedded in the GLB.

## Armour — 310 Armour Road headquarters

May 2015 panorama `SWxMbpBwp1WONNK9M5SOOQ` was also inspected as historical context.

New visible information beyond the previous cropped sign reference:
- A long low red/brown brick office elevation borders the driveway/parking area.
- There is a row of narrow upper windows and low, paired basement-level windows along the sloping lawn edge.
- A deep pale fascia runs above the brick; roof equipment is visible at a distance.
- A glazed entrance, steps/handrails, paved paths, broad asphalt parking and a small light-coloured gabled outbuilding are visible. Vegetation and parked vehicles obscure parts of the facade.

The camera viewpoint does not establish the ambulance garage door count, opening locations on unseen walls, or a full rear plan. Do not infer door counts from staffing/ambulance totals. Mapping this elevation to model-local edges needs care: the old contract's front assignment was inferred, not surveyed. This pass records the visible office details but does not move the GIS anchor or invent a garage arrangement.
