# Peterborough Fire Station 3 asset provenance

## Source and scope

This asset is original Blender geometry generated by `tools/blender/build_station_three.py`. It uses the City of Peterborough Fire Services exterior photograph and a second street photograph as visual references only. The images are not embedded, traced, projected, or used as material textures; no third-party meshes, textures, or external fonts are included. Text uses Blender's built-in font and is converted to geometry.

The revised street-facing treatment follows the verified photographs: a broad enamel-red entrance volume with fine dark cladding seams, two white-framed windows, a single glazed entry under a projecting canopy, a tall silver frame and upright crest panel above the canopy, compact station/address lettering on the right side of the red field, a stepped charcoal-brick bay wing, three pale-framed glazed apparatus doors with solid lower panels, a broad elevated charcoal bay canopy, and three distinguishable flag approximations. Varied mounded shrubs and flowers sit in curb-edged planting beds. Dimensions not established by the photographs remain visual approximations. A 2024 substantial-performance certificate confirms second-floor work but does not establish its location or layout; this asset does not infer a second-storey facade from that certificate.

Visual references: [City of Peterborough Fire Services photograph](https://www.peterborough.ca/media/55fdhfjh/peterborough-fire-services_2018_07_31_city_peterborough_2018_2.jpg) and [PtboCanada street photograph](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/50cca155e4b0c301a8212ad4/714fe747-bf30-4e88-81a8-a1c81b8804df/IMG_0347.JPG). The second-floor evidence is the [2024 substantial-performance certificate](https://canada.constructconnect.com/dcn/certificate-attachments/97417--2024-09-12_15-11-4324024-form9-Substantial-Performance.pdf); only the existence of second-floor work is established there, not its facade location.

The complete exterior footprint comes from OSM way 558402285 in `city-explorer/data/peterborough-osm.json`. The projected outline is about 1,656.9 m², with the measured southeast frontage about 28.2 m long. This derivative footprint geometry remains subject to OpenStreetMap attribution and ODbL obligations. Resolve those terms before commercial redistribution.

The interior has no floor-plan source. The apparatus hall divider, service room, lockers, benches, cabinets, equipment, and passage are clearly marked approximate scenery; they are not surveyed building facts. GIS remains authoritative for roads, terrain, navigation, collision, and station placement. The apron is a small visual frontage surface.

## Geographic frame

The model origin is the midpoint of the SE-facing edge of OSM way 558402285 at 44.28490065, -78.3506243. The frontage tangent runs northeast at approximately 35.37° from true north; the outward normal is southeast at 125.37°.

- Blender authoring axes: X east, Y north, Z up.
- GLB/Three.js axes: X east, Y up, Z south.
- The station root rotation is 54.63° about Blender Z (54.63° about GLB Y).
- The mapped outline is approximately 58.5 m wide along the facade axis and 38.0 m deep; its full projected extents and area are in the contract JSON.

## Bay and door contract

Each opening is 4.6 m wide and 4.2 m high. Bay centers in station-local frontage coordinates are -0.7 m, +5.0 m, and +10.7 m. The first 14 m behind the facade forms the approximate apparatus hall.

The movable parent nodes are `Station3_Door_01`, `Station3_Door_02`, and `Station3_Door_03`. Each parent pivot is centered at the bottom of its opening. Its horizontal panel nodes are `Station3_Door_##_Slat_00` through `Station3_Door_##_Slat_09`, ordered bottom to top. Door local axes are +X along the frontage northeast, +Y up, and +Z outward southeast.

For slat index `i` from 0 through 9, the closed Three.js local pose is position `(0, 0.21 + 0.42*i, 0)` with `rotation.x = 0`. The open endpoint is position `(0, 4.20, -(i + 0.5)*0.42)` with `rotation.x = -pi/2`. The controller should animate each child slat; translating only the parent would not roll the door overhead.

## Truck spawn

The locator `Station3_TruckSpawn_Bay01` is centered in Bay 01, the left-hand bay when viewed from outside, 5.6 m inward from the frontage. Relative to the GLB anchor it is X=-4.972 m, Y=0, Z=-2.671 m; negative GLB Z is north/inward. It faces outward at a game heading of 234.63° (4.0951 rad) when the vehicle's local forward axis is -Z.

For a 10.4 m long, 2.55 m wide truck, the nominal body clearance is 1.025 m per side in a 4.6 m opening. With the truck centered at depth 5.6 m, its front edge is 0.4 m behind the threshold and its rear edge is 3.2 m before the approximate 14 m hall divider. Mirror and steering clearance still depends on the runtime vehicle dimensions and should be checked in the driving view.
