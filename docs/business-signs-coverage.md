# Business signs coverage - v1.6.129

932 business names passed desktop and narrow-layout runtime checks: 900 storefront signs and 32 names on 4 shared-entrance directory panels. 1332 catalogue records remain unresolved. This is a public-source catalogue, not proof that every real-world business is known.

Source records: OpenStreetMap 1347; City of Peterborough 73; Chamber 33; DBIA/The Boro 290; ptbo.ca 785. Records with missing locations or unresolved duplicate identity remain in the audit; that count is not proof of additional physical businesses missing from the game. Each record retains source links and check dates. Directory pins require cross-source name/address corroboration or an exact municipal civic address on a property with one complete rendered building; conflicting or unsupported locations are retained in the unresolved audit. Raw footage, source images and raw directory markup are not published.

27 directory records have municipal civic-address/building corroboration. 635 storefronts use municipal property-address street evidence; 26 use an inferred nearest named street. Property primary addresses do not establish the exact tenant door.

Names are wall-mounted in the opening band. Ordinary doors/windows and unsurveyed tenant positions remain inferred. Mall directory panels identify businesses in the building, rather than implying individual exterior shop entrances; mapped ground-floor shared-door IDs and positions are retained. Panels keep their 4.8 metre reading width, wall bounds and roof clearance. Untagged mall floor heights use an explicitly inferred 4.8 metre commercial storey; explicit heights and footage-reviewed profiles take precedence.

Geometry/renderer regressions cover outward-facing text, neighbouring tenants, low roofs, map-mode round trips, editor hiding and shared entrance evidence. Front and both oblique approaches were captured across representative downtown and outlying buildings. Browser checks do not certify a physical phone. Shared atlas allocation: 20 MiB.

Data: `city-explorer/data/business-directory.json`; unresolved records: `business-directory.coverage.json`. The broad business-completeness goal remains open while those records and additional source coverage need evidence.
