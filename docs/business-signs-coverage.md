# Business signs coverage - v1.6.128

920 business names passed desktop and narrow-layout runtime checks: 888 storefront signs and 32 names on 4 shared-entrance directory panels. 495 catalogue records remain unresolved. This is a public-source catalogue, not proof that every real-world business is known.

Sources: OpenStreetMap, the City of Peterborough public business directory (all 73 entries), and obtainable Chamber category/search listings. Each record retains source links and check dates. Coarse City/Chamber pins require matching name and civic-address corroboration; conflicting or unsupported locations are retained in the unresolved audit. Raw footage, source images and raw directory markup are not published.

642 storefronts use municipal property-address street evidence; 26 use an inferred nearest named street. Property primary addresses do not establish the exact tenant door.

Names are wall-mounted in the opening band. Ordinary doors/windows and unsurveyed tenant positions remain inferred. Mall directory panels identify businesses in the building, rather than implying individual exterior shop entrances; mapped ground-floor shared-door IDs and positions are retained. Panels keep their 4.8 metre reading width, wall bounds and roof clearance. Untagged mall floor heights use an explicitly inferred 4.8 metre commercial storey; explicit heights and footage-reviewed profiles take precedence.

Geometry/renderer regressions cover outward-facing text, neighbouring tenants, low roofs, map-mode round trips, editor hiding and shared entrance evidence. Front and both oblique approaches were captured across representative downtown and outlying buildings. Browser checks do not certify a physical phone. Shared atlas allocation: 20 MiB.

Data: `city-explorer/data/business-directory.json`; unresolved records: `business-directory.coverage.json`. The broad business-completeness goal remains open while those records and additional source coverage need evidence.
