# Base driving area fit — v1.6.160

Reviewed satellite imagery against the actual runtime rectangles for all five Peterborough bases and eight Richmond Hill service entries. Peterborough already has fitted rectangles (including its previously authored Station 2 area; available imagery shows that site under construction). Preserve those dimensions and spawn settings. Richmond Hill canonical records had no yard dimensions and fell back to 160 m north-aligned squares.

Manually fitted Richmond Hill building, apron and parking coverage, with a small gameplay margin: Fire 8-1 98 x 65 m at 17 degrees; 8-2 76 x 95 at 17; 8-3 41 x 78 at 14; 8-4 63 x 54 at 17; shared Fire 8-5 / EMS 85 77 x 72 at 20; 8-6 63 x 85 at 17; EMS 28 96 x 136 at 17. Angles follow the base store's counterclockwise-from-east convention. These are visual gameplay fits, not surveyed parcel boundaries.

Keep source coordinates in geo-data unchanged. A separate drivingArea record defines the runtime area's center, dimensions, rotation and spawn. The shared package factory applies those fields, and the existing base store/editor can continue saving user changes. All existing spawn coordinates are preserved except EMS 28.

ArcGIS World Geocoding returned score 100 for this exact address at 43.86904730176, -79.442263934502. Satellite imagery shows the building and large north parking lot at that address. Fit the driving area around that site and use a modeled paved-apron spawn, not a verified ambulance bay. Retain the source point and explanatory note; no canonical call points or official facility metadata are changed.
